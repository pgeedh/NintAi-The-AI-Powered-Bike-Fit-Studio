Camera Alignment Protocol

1. Perpendicular Sagittal View (90°) Position lens directly side-on to the drive or non-drive side. Both wheel axles must align with minimum perspective yaw.
2. Camera Height & Distance Mount camera at bottom bracket / crank spindle level (~0.8m above ground) at a distance of 2.5–3.0 meters.
3. Lighting & Fitted Apparel High-contrast fitted bibs and cycling shoes. Ensure ankle bone, knee lateral epicondyle, and greater trochanter are visible.
