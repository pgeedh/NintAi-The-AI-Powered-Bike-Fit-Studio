    Camera Setup & Best Practices

    📐 90° Perpendicular Angle

    Place camera square to the bike frame. Both wheels should be equidistant from the lens.

    📏 Bottom Bracket Height

    Mount phone / tripod roughly level with your bike's bottom bracket or saddle line (~0.8m).

    👕 High-Contrast Kit

    Fitted cycling shorts and jersey ensure precise knee, hip, and ankle landmark detection.

    ⏱️ Steady 80–90 RPM

    Record 10–25 seconds of continuous pedalling at moderate resistance on a trainer or rollers.

    Pro Tip:

    The system auto-locks onto whichever side of your body is closest to the camera (left or right). No manual marker placement required!
